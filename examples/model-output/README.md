# Synthetic model outputs

Generated with seed 2026 and 48 independently generated fictional people. These are demonstration outputs, not the published study measurements or findings.

The archived notebook profile fitted 156 models: 84 questionnaire models and 72 AU models. The questionnaire fits reported convergence without warnings in this run. All 72 AU fits had a parameter-boundary warning; five AU fits also failed to converge. Their effects are marked `fit_review_required` and are not presented as usable significant effects. No estimator or source-study method was changed to suppress these warnings.

The three PNGs were visually inspected. All 416 exported raw/adjusted p-value pairs were checked against statsmodels correction using the documented families. See `docs/validation.md` for the environment and remaining scientific limitations.
